# Problem Statement

## Predictive Intelligence Engine

The Predictive Intelligence Engine is an AI-powered decision support system that analyzes historical data and domain knowledge to generate intelligent forecasts, risk assessments, and business recommendations.

### Key Problems

- **Data Overload:** Historical and real-time data is large, diverse, and difficult to analyze manually.
- **Reactive Decision-Making:** Many systems explain what happened but fail to predict what will happen next.
- **Fragmented Data Sources:** Useful insights are distributed across datasets, APIs, reports, and live feeds.
- **Limited Forecasting Accuracy:** Traditional methods can struggle with rapidly changing and complex patterns.
- **No Scenario Planning:** Decision-makers may not be able to simulate "What-if?" situations before taking action.

## Objective

The objective is to combine forecasting, scenario analysis, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and rule-based decision intelligence to support proactive and data-driven decisions.
